FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use everything as a guest with the same free limits. A free account (email and password) saves your exam results and chats to your account, so they’re there on any device and survive clearing your browser.

What does an account save, and who can see it?

Your exam scores, weak topics and reviewed attempts, plus your chats. They’re stored in a database protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change your rows. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and never reaches DocuChat’s server.

What happens to my documents?

Your documents are processed for your session only. The server keeps the extracted text and search index in memory for your workspace and does not write them to a database. The PDF file itself is saved in your own browser so it’s there when you come back; clear it anytime by removing the document. Text you ask about is sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate answers.

Which exam styles does Exam Prep support?

University (short-answer and essay-style theory), WAEC (objectives and structured theory), JAMB/UTME (four-option objectives) and a general style. Choose easy, medium, hard or mixed, a page range, and timed or untimed practice. Every question shows the page it came from.

Can it make things up?

It’s instructed to answer only from the retrieved passages and to say “I couldn’t find that in your documents” otherwise. Every claim is cited so you can check it in one click. AI can still misread a passage, so verify anything important.

Which PDFs work?

Any PDF with selectable text, up to 20 MB. Scanned images without a text layer need OCR first.

What counts as a question?

Each chat message and each tool run (summary, flashcards, quiz, brief, comparison) counts as one. Research briefs and citations count one per document. Exam Prep has its own allowance: 5 exam sets a day, and objective answers are marked instantly for free. If the AI fails, nothing is counted.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Chat and Exam Prep live in one app with a bottom tab bar on phones; the library and PDF viewer fold away on small screens.

Is it open source?

Yes, the code is on GitHub.
